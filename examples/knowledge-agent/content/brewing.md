# Brewing guide

## Pour-over

Use 15 grams of medium-fine coffee for 250 grams of water, a ratio of about 1 to 16. Heat the
water to 94 degrees Celsius. Wet the grounds with 40 grams of water, wait 30 seconds for the
bloom, then pour the rest in slow circles. The brew should finish in about three minutes.

## French press

Use 30 grams of coarse coffee for 500 grams of water just off the boil. Stir once, put the lid
on without plunging, and steep for four minutes. Plunge slowly and pour everything out right
away, because coffee left on the grounds keeps extracting and turns bitter.

## Cold brew

Combine 100 grams of coarse coffee with one litre of cold water in a jar. Leave it in the
fridge for 16 hours, then strain through a paper filter. The concentrate keeps for ten days
refrigerated. Dilute it one to one with water or milk before drinking.

## Storing beans

Keep beans in the bag they came in, sealed, in a cupboard away from the oven. Do not store
them in the fridge or freezer: condensation dulls the flavour. Beans taste best between one
and four weeks after the roast date printed on the bag.
