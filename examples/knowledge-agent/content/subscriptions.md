# Subscriptions

## Plans

A subscription delivers one or two 250 gram bags every two or four weeks. Subscribers pay 10
percent less than the shop price and never pay for shipping. Each delivery is a different
coffee unless you pin a favourite in your account.

## Pausing and skipping

You can skip a single delivery or pause the whole subscription for up to three months from
your account page. Changes made at least three days before the next roast day apply to that
delivery; later changes apply to the one after.

## Cancelling

Cancel at any time from your account page. There is no minimum term and no cancellation fee.
A delivery that has already been roasted still ships and is charged.

## Gift subscriptions

Gift subscriptions run for three, six or twelve deliveries and are paid up front. They do not
renew. The recipient chooses the grind and can pause deliveries like any other subscriber.
