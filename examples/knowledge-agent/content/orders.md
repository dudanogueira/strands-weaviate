# Orders and returns

## Shipping

Orders placed before noon ship the same working day. Delivery takes two to four working days.
Shipping is free on orders above 40 euros and costs 4.90 euros otherwise. We roast on Mondays
and Thursdays, so a bag never waits more than four days before it ships.

## Returns

Unopened bags can be sent back within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Opened bags
cannot be returned, but if a coffee is not to your taste, write to us within 14 days and we
will send a different one at no charge, once per customer.

## Damaged parcels

If a parcel arrives damaged, send a photo of the box and the bag to help@tidewater.example
within seven days. We replace the order without asking you to return anything.
